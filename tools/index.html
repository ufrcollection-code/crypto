A calculator should
clarify a decision.

Use the profit and DCA tools when you need cost-basis arithmetic. Use position size, risk-reward and forex margin when you are planning downside and exposure. Drawdown, dividend-yield and investment-fee tools cover different portfolio questions, while market cap and compound growth make longer-term assumptions visible.

Each calculator keeps inputs visible, runs locally in the browser and explains what the simple formula leaves out. No calculator value is sent to an account or trading venue. For a practical starting point, estimate a downside limit with the crypto position size calculator, then read the supporting guide before applying an unfamiliar result.
