About the academy

A quieter way to learn markets

CryptoStoks Academy is an independent learning project for people who want market concepts explained in plain language. We bring crypto, forex and stocks into one structured path while keeping the boundaries clear: education is not a personalised recommendation, a signal service or a promise of returns.

What we are building

The academy starts with evergreen foundations: how markets quote price, how orders work, how risk is measured and how to research an asset. Each lesson aims to move from concept to example to a small practice or calculator so the reader can check their understanding.

The site is designed for self-directed learners. It does not require a login, ask for a wallet deposit or connect to a brokerage account.

How we think about trust

We label illustrative data, separate education from opinion and link readers toward primary sources when a topic depends on current rules or company information. The platform is reviewed over time, but no page can remove the uncertainty of financial markets.

If you find a factual or accessibility issue, contact the team with the page URL and a clear description so it can be investigated.

Our editorial promise

We will not present guaranteed returns, hidden paid signals or urgency-led calls to action as education. Where a calculation uses simplifying assumptions, those assumptions should be visible on the page.
