Author / founder

Umar Farooq

Umar Farooq founded CryptoStocks Academy to make the language of crypto, forex and stocks easier to understand without signals, hype or guaranteed outcomes. He writes and reviews the academy’s beginner guides, worked examples and calculator explanations with a risk-first approach.
