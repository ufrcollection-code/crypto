Privacy policy

This policy describes the current behaviour of the static CryptoStocks Academy site and was reviewed on 28 September 2026. The site uses consent-controlled Google Analytics for audience measurement. The supplied build does not contain Google ad units or an AdSense publisher ID.

Visible limits.

A mostly static experience

The learning pages and calculators work without an account. Calculator inputs are processed in the browser and are not sent to a CryptoStocks Academy database. The paper trading simulator stores its sample portfolio in browser local storage; it is not a broker account or remote portfolio database.

The site also stores the visitor's analytics preference locally so the choice can be respected on later pages. Clearing site data removes the simulator record and privacy preference from that browser. The hosting provider may process technical request data such as IP address, user agent and timestamps under its own policies.

Google Analytics

CryptoStocks Academy uses Google Analytics 4 measurement ID G-D95T0FNX16 to understand aggregate page visits, device categories, traffic sources and interactions so the learning experience can be maintained. The site presents an analytics choice before loading the Google Analytics library. If a visitor continues without analytics, the analytics tag is not loaded by this site code.

If analytics is allowed, Google Analytics may use first-party identifiers such as the _ga cookie and process technical information under Google's terms and privacy controls. CryptoStocks Academy does not use this website to ask for a name, trading account, wallet address or payment information for analytics. Analytics reports are used for audience measurement and content improvement, not personalised financial advice.

Email and support

If you email the address listed on the Contact page, the email provider may retain the message and address so a reply can be sent. Do not include recovery phrases, private keys, passwords or payment details. Correspondence should be kept only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations.

Advertising status

The supplied site build does not contain Google ad units or an AdSense publisher ID. Applying to an advertising programme does not itself mean ads are serving. If advertising is enabled, this policy must be updated to describe the active provider, cookies or identifiers, data uses and available controls.

Before serving Google ads where regional consent rules apply, the publisher must configure an appropriate Google-certified consent management platform through AdSense or another certified provider. The site's current analytics choice is not presented as a replacement for an advertising CMP.

Your choices

Use the Privacy choices control in the footer to allow or decline the site's current analytics measurement. A visitor can change the choice later or clear browser storage. Browser settings can also block or remove cookies, although that can reset saved preferences and the local paper portfolio.

Google provides separate privacy information and controls for its services. Requirements vary by location, so this page describes the site configuration and is not a substitute for legal advice.

Consent and regional requirements

The analytics preference in this build controls the current Google Analytics tag. It does not represent an IAB Transparency and Consent Framework string and is not described as a Google-certified advertising CMP.

If Google ads are enabled, the publisher must configure the appropriate AdSense privacy message or another certified CMP for regions where Google requires one, then re-check this policy against the live configuration.
