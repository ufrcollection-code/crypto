Privacy policy

Effective date: 6 September 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how CryptoStoks Academy handles information when you visit cryptostoks.site, use its learning pages and calculators, or contact the team.

Visible limits.

Information the site collects

The current website is a static educational site. You do not need an account to read lessons, use calculators or open the paper-trading practice tool. Calculator entries are processed in your browser by the site code and are not intentionally sent to a CryptoStoks Academy server. The paper-trading tool may store its sample portfolio in your browser's local storage; it is not a user account or custody system.

When you visit the site, GitHub Pages, its infrastructure providers or a future hosting provider may process technical request information such as an IP address, browser or device type, user agent, requested page, referring page and request time. This information is handled under the provider's own privacy terms and may be used for delivery, security, abuse prevention and reliability.

Email and support messages

If you contact us at ufrcollection@gmail.com, we may receive your email address, message, attachments and any information you choose to include. We use this information to reply, investigate corrections, respond to accessibility reports, discuss partnerships or handle another request you initiated. Please do not send passwords, recovery phrases, private keys, payment details or other sensitive credentials.

Email messages are handled by the email provider and may remain in inboxes, backups or mail logs for as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain records or meet legal obligations. We do not sell contact information.

Cookies, Google AdSense and advertising

If advertising is enabled on this website, Google AdSense and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, web beacons, IP addresses and similar identifiers to deliver, measure and help prevent fraud in advertising. Depending on your location and your choices, ads may be contextual or personalised. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser when ads are displayed.

For information about Google's use of data when partner websites use Google services, visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can review or change Google advertising personalisation settings at https://adssettings.google.com. You can also review industry opt-out choices at https://optout.aboutads.info. These choices may not remove all advertising; they may change how ads are selected.

Where consent is required, including for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, advertising cookies or personalised advertising should be enabled only after the site's consent mechanism records the relevant choice. You can change or withdraw your consent through the consent controls provided on the site, where available.

Other third-party services

The site may link to or later use third-party services such as GitHub Pages, an email provider, Google advertising services, embedded media or external research sources. Those providers may process information under their own policies. We do not control third-party websites, their cookies or their security practices. This policy will be updated if a new service materially changes what the site collects or shares.

Your choices and privacy requests

You may choose not to contact us, clear browser local storage through your browser settings, block or delete cookies, and use available advertising or consent controls. You may email ufrcollection@gmail.com to ask what personal information we hold about your correspondence, request correction or ask for deletion where applicable. We may need to verify the request and may retain information where required for legal, security or record-keeping reasons.

Children, security and policy changes

CryptoStoks Academy is general financial education and is not directed at children. Do not use the site to submit a child's personal information. We use reasonable measures appropriate to a static website, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy when the site, advertising, analytics or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will appear at the top of this page. If a material change is made, the updated policy will be published here before the changed practice is used.

Contact

Questions, privacy requests and correction requests can be sent to ufrcollection@gmail.com. Website: https://cryptostocks.site/.
