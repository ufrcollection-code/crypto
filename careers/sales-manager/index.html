Sales Manager.

Fully remoteCryptoStocks Academy

Help organise relevant business enquiries and partnership conversations for CryptoStocks Academy. This role focuses on communication and a reliable sales process, rather than selling investments or promising financial returns.

Apply for this role ↗

The contribution

What you will work on.

  • Research relevant business prospects and organise an outreach pipeline.
  • Handle enquiries, qualification and follow-ups professionally.
  • Keep accurate notes on conversations, next steps and opportunities.
  • Coordinate proposals and communicate agreed requirements clearly.
  • Report activity and outcomes honestly without inflated claims.

Your strengths

What we are looking for.

  • Clear written and spoken communication with a professional manner.
  • Relevant experience in sales, business development or relationship management.
  • Comfort using spreadsheets or a CRM to organise work.
  • Good judgement about truthful claims, permissions and appropriate outreach.
