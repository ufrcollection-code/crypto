How to report a correction

Please include the exact page URL, the heading or sentence you are referring to, what appears incorrect or unclear, and a reliable source or explanation where available. Screenshots can help when the issue is visual, technical or accessibility-related.

Specific reports allow the editorial team to reproduce the issue, check the relevant source and decide whether a correction, clarification or technical fix is appropriate. A submitted suggestion is reviewed, but publication of a change is not guaranteed.

What we can and cannot help with

We can help with questions about the site's educational explanations, calculator assumptions, paper-trading practice, broken links, accessibility and general content feedback. We can also receive proposals for educational references or clearly disclosed partnerships.

We cannot provide personalised investment, financial, tax or legal advice, predict a market's next move, execute trades, manage funds, recover crypto, troubleshoot a third-party account or guarantee a result. Please use the site's Risk Disclosure before applying an example to a real decision.

Partnerships and source references

If you represent a data source, educator or research organisation, include your organisation name, website, the page you are referring to and the relationship you are proposing. Explain whether the proposal is editorial, commercial, sponsored or purely informational.

Any commercial relationship, affiliate link or sponsored placement must be evaluated for relevance and disclosed clearly before publication. A partnership request does not guarantee a link, mention or endorsement.
