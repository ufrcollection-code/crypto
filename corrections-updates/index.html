How to request a correction

Email the academy with the exact page URL, the heading or statement to review, an explanation of the issue and a primary source where one is available. Calculator reports should include the inputs, displayed output and expected result. Do not send passwords, recovery phrases, account credentials or payment details.

A report is checked against the page's assumptions and appropriate sources. A material correction should update the affected explanation, example, formula or link and receive a new review date. Minor spelling or layout fixes do not automatically change an article's substantive review date.

What an update date means

A visible update date should indicate a meaningful review of the page's explanation, assumptions, calculation or sources. It should not be changed merely to make content appear fresh. Time-sensitive rules, fees, product details and regulations should still be verified at their current primary source.
